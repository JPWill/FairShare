# FairShare rules

**What we do.** Every boss kill belongs to everyone who was there. Loot is auctioned for **shares**, our own points, and the price is paid back out to the raid. Your shares follow you across every character. No DKP tables, no loot council, no favours.

1. **One person, one account.** Register every character and your Battle.net tag to your Discord. Points live on you, not a character. A second Discord account is a ban.
2. **Loot is auctioned for shares, never gold.** Want an item? Bid what it is worth to you. Bids are sealed; the winner pays the second-highest bid, or the floor price if nobody else bid (10 shares for any item, 20 for a two-handed weapon). Gold never changes hands.
3. **Everyone gets paid.** 90% of every sale is split evenly among everyone in the raid, standby included. 10% goes to the night's performance pool.
4. **Bids are final.** Credit lets you go negative up to your limit, which grows with every raid you attend. Earn it back by raiding.
5. **Off-spec is fine.** Tick the box when you bid. The leader may set a discount for the night.
6. **Won it, wear it.** The item goes to the winner. The raid leader can transfer it to another raider during the raid at the same price. Trading loot any other way is a ban.
7. **Stay connected during auctions.** Your addon unseals your bid automatically when bidding closes. If that never arrives (a disconnect), you get one free pass per raid; after that it costs a penalty, because a bid that stays hidden is the one way to game the result.
8. **Be there.** Every sale pays out to everyone present, standby included. Each boss kill you attend, and each full run, also counts toward your boss kills, which decide invite priority. Going AFK earns nothing.
9. **Run the addon.** No addon, no bids. The leader runs a test auction before the first boss; fix anything it flags.
10. **Everything is checked.** Every raider's addon verifies every auction and keeps the full record. The leader uploads it and shares move. Think something is off? Upload your own copy from the addon, any time: if it disagrees, the raid is frozen for the officers, whose call is final.

Cheating, bid rings, loot trading, and alt accounts get you banned on every character you have ever linked.
